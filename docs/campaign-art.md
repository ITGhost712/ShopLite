# ShopLite campaign artwork

Both photographs were created with built-in Image Gen, then encoded as JPEGs for the app bundle. The banners illustrate the collections. The product pages use the catalog's own photos.

## The Scent Edit

[Bundled photograph](../assets/campaign/amber-fragrance.jpg)

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium shopping app Home campaign banner photography. Create an arresting photorealistic luxury fragrance still life, a sculptural unbranded amber-glass perfume bottle with a black cap on dark split basalt, tiny glints of copper, soft black background and honey-toned directional light. Horizontal 3:2 composition, bottle in the upper right half, retain breathing room on edges for responsive cropping, keep the lower quarter and lower left dark and quiet for separately rendered app text. Crisp glass edges, believable reflections, premium editorial photography. Palette exclusively warm amber, copper, coffee, cream and neutral charcoal. No purple, text, logos, UI, borders or watermarks.

## The Living Edit

[Bundled photograph](../assets/campaign/walnut-lounge.jpg)

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium shopping app Home campaign banner photography. Create a photorealistic luxury interiors editorial showing a single sculptural low walnut lounge chair with cream bouclé upholstery and generous curves, on a charcoal stone floor. A shaft of warm daylight falls across the chair and reveals tactile fabric and walnut grain. Horizontal 3:2 composition, chair mostly center-right, keep the lower quarter and lower left dark and quiet for app text. Rich shadows, razor-sharp detail, high-end interiors photography, strictly warm charcoal, walnut, caramel and warm cream, no purple. No text, logos, UI, borders or watermarks.
